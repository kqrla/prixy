prixy less as “another ai meeting assistant” and more as a personal presence proxy. the important distinction is that prixy is not primarily trying to make meetings easier to consume. it is trying to make it possible for you to not be there without losing the specific things you needed from being there.

here’s how i’d structure the landing/about/philosophy copy.

prixy

your presence, when you can't be there.

príxy is a personal ai proxy that can show up to things on your behalf.

it can register for events, join meetings, handle attendance, listen for information you care about, ask questions you gave it, and bring back exactly what you needed.

not a meeting recap.

not another productivity dashboard.

not a 47-minute transcript turned into a 900-word summary you never asked for.

príxy goes instead of you.

the problem

maybe you didn't need a meeting summary.

there's been a strange assumption in productivity software that the hardest part of a meeting is remembering what happened.

so we got:

ai meeting summaries
meeting recaps
action items
highlights
key takeaways
follow-up summaries

and sometimes those are useful.

but there's a very different problem that gets overlooked:

what if you didn't need a summary because you were already there?

if you sat through the meeting, you already know what happened.

you don't need software to explain your own experience back to you.

and if you're someone who already struggles with executive function, attention, working memory, or information overload, being handed more content isn't necessarily assistance.

sometimes it's just another thing to process.

the other problem

sometimes you shouldn't have to be there at all.

the useful information might be buried inside the event.

maybe there's a question you need answered.

maybe a discount code is only announced live.

maybe attendance matters.

maybe the organizer takes questions at the end.

maybe the meeting is at 4am in your timezone.

maybe you're double-booked.

maybe you simply don't want to spend an hour listening to something when you only need one specific piece of information from it.

that's where a proxy makes more sense than a recap.

so what does prixy do?

prixy goes.

you tell it where to go and what you need.

príxy can:

register for events on your behalf

join supported virtual meetings

handle attendance when applicable

follow along with the session

ask questions you've specified

wait for the appropriate moment to ask them

capture the answers

retrieve specific information from the session

send you the information you actually asked for

retain the transcript and supporting material for later inspection

the important part is the last one:

you don't have to consume everything just because prixy collected everything.

information, not content

prixy doesn't assume you want a recap.

say there's an online event at 4am.

you tell prixy:

“go to this event and find the promo code they announce during the live session.”

príxy attends.

it listens.

it finds the code.

and you get:

promo code: example20

that's it.

you don't wake up to:

“good morning! here's your comprehensive 1,847-word summary of last night's event…”

because you didn't ask for that.

the transcript can still be retained for you on the server if you want to inspect it later.

the raw information stays available without forcing you to read it.

if you actually weren't there

that's different.

if prixy attended a meeting instead of you, a transcript can be useful.

a summary might be useful too.

but even then, prixy can work from your actual needs rather than assuming every meeting deserves the same “meeting recap” treatment.

you might want:

“tell me what decisions were made.”

or:

“ask whether the api will be available next month.”

or:

“find out what the application deadline is.”

or:

“tell me anything they said about pricing.”

príxy can treat the meeting as a source of information, rather than turning the entire thing into another piece of content for you to consume.

built for delegation, not productivity theater

a lot of productivity software is built around the idea that you should become better at personally processing everything.

príxy takes the opposite approach.

delegate the presence.

you shouldn't necessarily have to:

open the event page
register
remember the date
wake up at 4am
join the meeting
sit through the whole thing
watch the chat
remember the question
wait for the q&a
write down the answer
save the transcript
summarize everything afterward

if what you actually needed was:

“find out x.”

then the system should be able to handle the rest.

especially when your brain doesn't work like a productivity blog

more information isn't always more helpful.

for people with adhd, executive dysfunction, or simply limited attention, “we made more information available” isn't automatically a win.

a meeting recap can become:

another notification
another document
another wall of text
another thing to skim
another thing to remember
another thing to turn into a task

and now the productivity tool has created a new productivity task:

process the productivity tool's output.

príxy is designed around a simpler principle:

if you know what you need, give me that.

the anti-recap

príxy isn't fundamentally a summarizer.

it's a retrieval and presence system.

there's a difference.

a summarizer asks:

“what happened?”

a proxy asks:

“what did you need from what happened?”

and sometimes the answer is:

“nothing. i was there.”

in that case, príxy doesn't need to generate a recap at all.

your little proxy

príxy is personal by design.

it's not supposed to be a generic corporate meeting bot that gets invited to every calendar event.

it's your instance.

you give it instructions.

you decide what it should care about.

you decide what it should report back.

you can inspect the underlying transcript when you want the full context.

otherwise, it stays out of the way.

quietly present where you aren't.

philosophy

presence is expensive. information doesn't have to be.

being somewhere and knowing something are two different things.

príxy separates them.

you don't always need to spend an hour of your life acquiring one piece of information.

you don't always need to read a summary of something you already experienced.

you don't always need another productivity layer between yourself and the thing you actually wanted.

sometimes you just need someone to go.

that's what príxy is for.

about

a personal proxy, not another productivity app.

príxy started from a pretty simple question:

what if an ai assistant actually went somewhere instead of telling you about somewhere you already went?

the result is a personal agent that can act as your presence across supported digital spaces.

it can register.

it can attend.

it can listen.

it can ask.

it can retrieve.

and it can come back with exactly what you needed.

no forced recap.

no mandatory dashboard.

no assumption that every meeting needs to become content.

just a proxy.

possible homepage hero lines

option 1

you don't always need a meeting summary. sometimes you need someone to go.

option 2

your presence, delegated.

option 3

go to the meeting. get the thing.

option 4

an ai proxy for the things you don't need to personally attend.

option 5

stop attending things just to get one piece of information.

option 6

príxy goes so you don't have to.

and i think “you don't always need a meeting summary. sometimes you need someone to go.” is probably the strongest expression of the whole concept, because it immediately distinguishes prixy from the current “ai meeting assistant” category without needing to explain the technology first.
